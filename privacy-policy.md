# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction
We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who interacts with our Discord bot application. This Privacy Policy details the general practices we employ regarding the temporary processing and management of data to keep the application functional and secure.

## 2. Data We Process and Use
To deliver core utility features and maintain server safety, the bot handles specific technical markers during the following activities:

* Command Execution and Navigation: When you trigger the bot via text prefixes, slash commands, or interactive buttons, we process essential technical IDs, specifically your Discord Guild ID, User ID, Role ID, and Channel ID. This data is utilized solely to map and execute your operational requests.

* Welcome and Goodbye Images: When a member joins or leaves the server, the bot reads the User ID, Username, and Avatar URL asset temporarily. This metadata is held briefly in volatile memory for the sole purpose of dynamic-rendering personalized welcome or farewell image cards for the community.

* Audio and Music Player: When you stream tracks using the music feature, the bot processes the specific Track ID or song URL requested, alongside the Voice Channel ID to stream the audio stream accurately.

* Automated Moderation and Security Logs: To protect servers from malicious spam, raiders, and harmful links, the bot requires access to the real-time message text string. This text data is analyzed instantly to identify violations, and the raw payload is printed as concrete evidence inside the server's private administrator log channel for audit trail transparency.

## 3. Data Storage and Safety
* Volatile Processing: Any chat or message content evaluated by our security modules is processed strictly in-memory during real-time filtering. We do not maintain long-term persistent storage or external databases for your server's message histories.
* Security Measures: We enforce industry-standard encryption tokens and secure hosting environments to protect all handled data from unauthorized access or digital leaks.

## 4. Zero Third-Party Sharing
None of the data processed by the bot is ever shared with, sold to, or distributed to any external third-party services or advertising networks. Information is exclusively presented back to authorized server personnel through standard interactive bot features, such as automated log cards or playback dashboards.
